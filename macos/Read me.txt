LOCAL MEETING NOTES — macOS 15+ / APPLE SILICON

RUN THE BUILT APP
Download the Mac ZIP from this private repository's Releases page and move Local Meeting Notes.app to Applications before first opening it. The app contains its offline model, native Whisper helper and framework; running it needs no Xcode, account, transcription service or model download.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. Follow your device's application policies. The first native Apple Silicon CI build passed compilation, signature verification, storage/recovery checks, generated two-hour audio handling and model initialization. A headless runner cannot verify your live microphone/system-audio capture or permission prompts.

FIRST RECORDING
1. Open the app and choose German or English.
2. Choose your microphone, Default Mac microphone, or Off.
3. Click Start recording. Grant microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording access yourself when macOS asks; reopen the app if macOS requires it.
4. Use headphones, play a short system-audio sample and speak a few words. Check both level meters.
5. Press Stop and transcribe, then verify both sources appear in the transcript before relying on the app for a real meeting.

Only audio outputs are registered for recording; the app does not save video or screenshots. System capture can include notifications and other system sounds. Speech transcription stays on the Mac. Metal acceleration is attempted on Apple Silicon, with CPU initialization as a fallback when possible.

FILES AND RECOVERY
Recordings and notes are stored outside the app bundle:
~/Library/Application Support/LocalMeetingNotes/Meetings
Use Open meeting folder to find them. Moving or rebuilding the app does not intentionally remove that folder.

Audio writes continuously to disk with requested checkpoints about every five seconds. The app asks the OS to stay awake, checks free space and stops after eight hours or when space is running low. Two hours with system and microphone audio uses approximately 690 MB across the source and mixed WAV files. Keep at least 1 GB free before recording, with extra space for longer meetings or recovery copies.

Recover recording copies source tracks into a new folder, repairs stale WAV headers on those copies, mixes the tracks and transcribes. Originals remain unchanged; active recording locks prevent recovery while capture is still writing. Failed or cancelled transcription keeps the audio. Forced sleep, forced quit, device disconnects, power loss or storage damage can still lose recent or unavailable audio; no zero-loss guarantee is possible.

DELETION
Audio is kept by default. Once complete, nonempty transcript and subtitle files have been saved, Delete audio offers permanent deletion of system.wav, microphone.wav and meeting.wav in the CURRENT folder after your confirmation. Text, subtitles, pending text and logs stay. Imported originals and original interrupted-recording folders stay. This cannot be undone and is not secure disk erasure.

BUILD FROM THE REPOSITORY
Use an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 15+ and Xcode 16+ or compatible Command Line Tools containing the macOS 15 SDK. From the repository root run:
bash macos/Build.command --long-tests

The first build fetches checksum-verified model and framework files. Subsequent builds can reuse them. The script runs storage/recovery checks, builds and signs the app, verifies the signature and initializes the bundled model. The generated app and distribution ZIP are in macos/dist. If compilation fails, inspect macos/.build/build.log.

The build script defaults to local ad-hoc signing. You can provide your own SIGNING_IDENTITY for a Developer ID signature; notarization is a separate step. Apple SDKs are not redistributed. See the repository README and included third-party license notices for additional details.
